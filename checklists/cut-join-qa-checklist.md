# Cut and Join QA Checklist

- [ ] Every applied cut maps to an approved or policy-authorized proposal.
- [ ] Word and silence evidence supports the cut range.
- [ ] Protected facts, qualifications, names, numbers, warnings, and uncertain words remain.
- [ ] Useful breaths and intentional pauses remain.
- [ ] The selected take is complete, correct, and visually compatible.
- [ ] The join strategy is explicit.
- [ ] Neither side contains a clipped syllable or consonant.
- [ ] No click, pop, room-tone jump, or sudden noise-floor change is audible.
- [ ] No duplicate or missing word appears after re-transcription.
- [ ] The resulting sentence remains grammatical and logically coherent.
- [ ] Speech cadence is concise without sounding rushed.
- [ ] No black flash, frozen frame, duplicate frame, or very short retained fragment appears.
- [ ] Face, body, gesture, cursor, and screen state remain understandable.
- [ ] Required B-roll, punch-in, J-cut, L-cut, or room tone covers a difficult join.
- [ ] Failed checks route to repair or focused review.
- [ ] A high local cut count is accepted when these checks pass.
