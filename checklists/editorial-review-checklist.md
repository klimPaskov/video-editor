# Editorial Review Checklist

- [ ] Every semantic cut has a clear reason.
- [ ] Deleted content does not change meaning or chronology.
- [ ] Numbers, dates, names, quotations, and disclosures remain correct.
- [ ] Negation and qualifications remain intact.
- [ ] Pauses still sound natural.
- [ ] Cut boundaries do not clip words or breaths unnaturally.
- [ ] Captions match approved speech.
- [ ] Emphasis is limited and consistent with the brand.
- [ ] Motion graphics clarify the point.
- [ ] Sound cues support the edit and preserve speech clarity.
- [ ] B-roll matches narration and does not imply false evidence.
- [ ] Generated visuals are free of obvious artifacts and conflicting text.
- [ ] Hook and pacing fit the intended audience without over-editing.
- [ ] Final preview hash and QA report match the approval.
- [ ] Every zoom has a clear allowed purpose and visible target.
- [ ] Zoom timing begins after the target appears and ends before unrelated content.
- [ ] The actual target is centered and the motion is smooth, stable, and free of empty edges.
- [ ] No zoom was added where no clear target exists.
- [ ] Every speed-up was explicitly requested.
- [ ] Every speed-up contains only visible prompt writing or dictation.
- [ ] Browsing, reading, waiting, loading, navigation, result inspection, cursor wandering, and unrelated actions remain at normal speed.
- [ ] Sped-up production audio remains audible and synchronized unless an explicit exception was approved.
- [ ] Material uncertainty was batched with a recommendation and low-impact uncertainty used a safe fallback.

## Version 2.2 smart editing

- [ ] The editor found useful small edits throughout the segment.
- [ ] Obvious high-confidence micro edits were not left merely to reduce cut count.
- [ ] Filler and breath decisions preserve natural delivery.
- [ ] Repetition review distinguishes shared meaning from unique detail.
- [ ] Take selection uses quality evidence and does not assume the final take is best.
- [ ] Every motion transition marks a real new point or major visual change.
- [ ] Routine cleanup cuts remain visually simple.
- [ ] Transition sound supports movement without hiding dialogue.
