# Transition and Sound QA Checklist

- [ ] The transition marks a real structural boundary.
- [ ] Routine dialogue cleanup did not receive decorative motion.
- [ ] Outgoing and incoming segments are correct.
- [ ] The transition starts and ends on the approved output range.
- [ ] Direction supports the visual change and brand language.
- [ ] Easing is smooth with no shake, snap, drift, or edge gap.
- [ ] Incoming content is readable when the transition finishes.
- [ ] The first important incoming word remains clear.
- [ ] The sound asset is licensed and hash verified.
- [ ] The sound transient aligns with visual movement.
- [ ] Gain, fades, and ducking protect speech and avoid clipping.
- [ ] The same transition and sound are not repeated too often.
- [ ] The clean-cut fallback renders correctly.
- [ ] Low-confidence or weak-purpose proposals use the fallback.
